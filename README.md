### 👨‍💻 About Me

I have been passionate about programming since I was 17, when I entered college. I started my journey with Pascal and Borland C++. During my work in healthcare, I continued to research and learn ways to automate processes in my work environment, first using MS Excel and then MS Access. At the age of 26, I had the pleasure of learning PHP, a language that completely captivated me. Finally, at the age of 30, I made the full transition to the IT field, alternating between frontend development and online community management. I have always been driven by building elegant solutions and improving the web development ecosystem.

* 🌍 **Location:** Bogotá, Colombia 🇨🇴
* 🖥️ **Portfolio:** [emamut.vercel.app](https://emamut.vercel.app/)
* ✉️ **Contact:** [fabervergara@gmail.com](mailto:fabervergara@gmail.com)
